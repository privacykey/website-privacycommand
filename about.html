About privacycommand.

A small privacykey project and a forensic permission auditor for macOS, sibling to privacytracker.

privacykey

privacycommand is a privacykey project. The maintainers are visible on GitHub.

It exists because macOS already shows you the prompts an app asks for, but nothing tells you what's inside the bundle once it's installed, like the embedded analytics SDKs, hard-coded domains, launch agents, the live file and network events the app produces on a normal run. Pulling all of that together used to take an afternoon and a stack of command-line tools. privacycommand runs every detector against every drop and explains every finding in plain English, so the report is shareable with someone who isn't a binary-analysis nerd.

The app is free and open source under the MIT licence, runs entirely on your Mac, and ships zero analytics. The full code is at github.com/privacykey/privacycommand.

For bugs or feature ideas, open an issue. For security concerns, please follow SECURITY.md rather than a public report.
